Expansion & Product Roadmap

Following our MVP success, our SaaS pipeline focuses on connecting complete financial chains directly inside Unifyte.

Vector 01: Q3 2026

Payment Settlement Reconciliation

Direct linking with Razorpay, Paytm, and Stripe webhooks. Unifyte will monitor API networks and mark invoices as PAID automatically upon credit receipt without business owners needing to touch a single button.

Vector 02: Q4 2026

Automated Reminders Sequencing

Trigger automated email, SMS, and WhatsApp sequences directly to customers with overdue balances. Set custom scheduling timers (e.g. net-30 alert at 3 days remaining, 1 day remaining, and 3 days overdue) to secure healthy cash streams.

Vector 03: Q1 2027

Multi-Currency & Regionalization

Support customized localized taxes beyond Indian GST. Including complete multi-currency settlements, VAT, and US Sales Tax formatting to allow businesses to supply cross-border clients globally.

Vector 04: Q2 2027

Predictive ML Business Coach

Deep implementation of advanced predictive cash forecasting systems. Receive warnings when procurement costs spike, alerts when seasonal stocks will be in high demand, and flags of clients showing structural invoice default threats.
